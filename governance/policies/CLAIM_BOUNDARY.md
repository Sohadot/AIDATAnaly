---

## 1. Principle

AIDATAnaly earns reference status through precision, not assertion. This document is the single central list of what the asset may and may not claim, in any channel. Page-level enforcement lives in `scripts/validate-pages.ps1`; this policy is the authority the validator implements.

This repository does not claim industry adoption, guaranteed revenue improvement, or causal proof.

---

## 2. Allowed Language

- governed reference system
- AIDA Transition Analytics
- diagnostic reference layer
- transition movement / movement between AIDA states
- AIDA Transition Index (ATI) — *introduced by AIDATAnaly*
- Transition Failure Ontology (TFO) — *introduced by AIDATAnaly*
- Evidence Confidence
- rules-governed Transition Scanner
- "AIDATAnaly introduces…", "AIDATAnaly defines…", "AIDATAnaly publishes…"

## 3. Prohibited Language

- industry standard / adopted standard / official standard (as a claim about ATI, TFO, or the asset)
- proven revenue uplift / guaranteed revenue
- market adoption (as an established fact)
- causal proof
- guaranteed diagnosis / guaranteed outcome
- AI certainty ("AI knows exactly…")
- any engagement, traffic, or revenue figure presented as evidence inside public content

Prohibited terms may appear **only** inside negations, prohibition lists, or governance documents that restrict them — never as positive claims.

## 4. Correct Framing Examples

| Instead of | Write |
|------------|-------|
| "ATI is the industry standard" | "AIDATAnaly introduces the AIDA Transition Index" |
| "This will increase your revenue" | "Scanner outputs are diagnostic references, not guarantees of commercial outcome" |
| "Widely adopted by marketers" | "A governed framework published for humans and AI agents" |
