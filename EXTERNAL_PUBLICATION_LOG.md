## Purpose

This log records every external publication that binds the name **AIDAtanaly** to the category **AIDA Transition Analytics**. It is the evidence trail for closing gap **G1** (external footprint) of `INEVITABILITY_AUDIT.md` and for inevitability criteria **C1** (search resolution) and **C3** (external citation).

An unrecorded publication does not exist for governance purposes. A recorded publication converts external activity into auditable asset history.

---

## Log Rules

1. **Append-only.** Entries are never edited after the interaction window closes; corrections are appended as new lines marked "Correction".
2. **Positioning compliance.** Every entry must conform to `POSITIONING_BRIEF.md` before publication: first mention is always **AIDAtanaly — AIDA Transition Analytics**; the name is never used as a verb; gap-first hook.
3. **Public data only.** Interaction figures recorded here are limited to counts the platform displays publicly (reactions, comments, impressions where publicly visible). No private analytics, audience data, or personal information may enter this log (per `INEVITABILITY_AUDIT.md` §6).
4. **One entry per publication.** Each entry gets a stable ID `EPL-NNN`.
5. **Interaction snapshots are dated.** Early interaction is recorded as a dated snapshot, not a rolling figure.
