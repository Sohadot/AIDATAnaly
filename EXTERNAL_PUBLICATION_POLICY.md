**Repository artifact only** — not a public website route

---

## 1. Principle

This is a category artifact repository, not a marketing diary.

External publication evidence — post URLs, engagement counts, screenshots, analytics — is maintained **privately by the operator**, off-repository. The repository records governance, not volatility.

---

## 2. What This Repository Records

- approved positioning language,
- claim boundaries,
- publication status at a high level,
- public-facing messaging constraints.

## 3. What This Repository Does Not Store

- weak or early engagement metrics,
- non-material social screenshots,
- private analytics,
- volatile impression counts,
- public social links, unless they become materially relevant (e.g., a widely cited reference thread), in which case inclusion requires a governance decision.

---

## 4. Publication Rules (Unchanged in Substance)

1. **Positioning compliance.** Every external publication must conform to `POSITIONING_BRIEF.md` before it goes live: first mention is always **AIDATAnaly — AIDA Transition Analytics**; the name is never used as a verb; gap-first hook.
2. **Claims restraint.** All external copy is bound by `governance/policies/CLAIM_BOUNDARY.md`.
3. **High-level status only.** After a publication goes live, the operator updates the status register below — status and period only, no URLs, no counts.
4. **Private evidence file.** Detailed evidence (URLs, dated interaction snapshots, screenshots) lives in the operator's private records and is available to a qualified acquirer under disclosure, not in this repository.

## 5. Creative Assets Rule

Public **site** assets are the only images that belong in this repository. Social post creatives (LinkedIn/X images, banners, thumbnails) stay outside the repository unless and until they become part of the official website, in which case they enter under `assets/` through the normal quality gate.

## 7. Relationship to the Inevitability Audit

Gap **G1** progress is tracked here at status level. The inevitability criteria (C1 search resolution, C3 external citation) are satisfied by *external* observable facts — rankings, citations, agent behavior — not by anything stored in this repository. The repository's role is to prove the publications were governed, not to archive their engagement.
